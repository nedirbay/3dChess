    Rules of the duel

    • Standard FIDE movement for all six pieces.
    • Castling — move the king two squares toward a rook. Allowed only if neither has moved, the path is clear and the king does not start in, pass through or land in check.
    • En passant — capture a pawn that just advanced two squares past yours.
    • Promotion — a pawn reaching the last rank becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight.
    • Checkmate wins. Stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material are draws.
